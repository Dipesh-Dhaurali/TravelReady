Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. How we collect, safeguard, and respect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal details such as your name, contact phone number, email address, and passport or identification data when required for issuing airline tickets, hotel reservations, and government trek permits.

Your credentials, payment transaction slips, and contact information are encrypted in transit and securely stored. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with external advertising networks.

We use your contact details solely for booking updates, ticket delivery, quotation alerts, and customer service follow-ups related to your journeys.

4. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For inquiries regarding your personal data or to request account information deletion, contact us at privacy@travelready.com.
