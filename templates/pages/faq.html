Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, customized routes, payment verification, and safety.

How do I book a tour package?

Browse through our Packages section, select your desired trip, pick your travel date and group size, and click Book Now. You'll be taken to our secure checkout where you can confirm details and make your payment.

How does the "Build My Trip" custom designer work?

Our Trip Builder allows you to select multiple destinations, choose your hotel tier (Budget, 3-Star, Luxury), add activities and select private or shared transport. It calculates your estimated total cost live and lets you save your draft or request an official quotation with 1 click.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support eSewa, Khalti, direct Bank Transfers with receipt upload, and standard credit/debit cards. Once submitted, our accounting team reviews the receipt and marks your booking verified and confirmed in your dashboard.

Where do I find my digital itineraries and vouchers?

Log in to your User Dashboard and navigate to Digital Itineraries or My Bookings. You can view your detailed daily schedule and download complete PDF travel itineraries right to your device.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellations made 14 days or more before the departure date receive a full refund minus minimal transaction fees. For cancellations made within 7-13 days, a 50% refund applies. Please refer to our Terms of Service page for complete terms.

Our support team is available around the clock to assist you.
